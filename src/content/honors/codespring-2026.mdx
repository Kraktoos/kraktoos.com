At my first hackathon, my team built **Ark** with <Logo fixed src="expo" /> **React Native / Expo** and later released it as an open-source project.

Ark keeps downloaded maps, guides, wiki archives, and weather available without an internet connection. It also has encrypted notes, document storage, and device tools such as a compass and coordinates.

The AI assistant runs on the phone and searches local content using **llama.rn**, embedding models, **SQLite FTS**, and **sqlite-vec**. Maps use **MapLibre**, while **SQLCipher** protects private data.

[Explore Ark and its documentation](https://ark.rgo.pt/) or [view the source on GitHub](https://github.com/rodrgds/ark).

### What I learned

We spent too much time debugging edge cases that judges never saw. Next time, I would polish the core flow and demo earlier, then expand the feature set.
